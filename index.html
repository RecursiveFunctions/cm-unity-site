The mission

Everyone at CMU becomes friends with each other.

We mean everyone: students, staff, faculty, and eventually administration. It reaches past campus too, with CMU people befriending people outside CMU.

A community with strong ties can do more, and do it better, than a loose one. So we build the ties first.

How the mission works

Three pillars.

Everything we do falls under three of them.

Friendship

Reshaping the social network

Building friendships across the groups that normally never cross paths, until "us" means all of CMU, and then more.

Discourse

Rehabilitating the flow of information

More frequent, longer, more honest conversation about philosophy, politics, religion, culture, and the human condition. It lives in our Discord and our General Body Meetings.

Kindness & care

Direct charity

Genuine kindness, and a real share of our work that is plain, direct care for the people around us.

The discourse pillar, in practice

Where the conversation happens.

Our Discord

The conversation is always open. Philosophy in the afternoon, politics at 2am. Come in, argue honestly, and stick around. It's the easiest way to start.

General Body Meetings

Everyone in one room, on a regular schedule. The same conversations, in person, with the people you're becoming friends with. Times and places go out in the Discord.
